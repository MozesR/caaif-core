# Community Conduct Guidelines — CAAIF

Be respectful and constructive. Disagreement about technical design is welcome; personal harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized harassment, publishing private information, and sustained disruption are not.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through an appropriately configured confidential channel (to be established before a public community is launched). Maintainers should acknowledge, investigate fairly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and apply proportionate actions up to removing content or access.

This is an initial project-specific policy, not a copy of a third-party code-of-conduct license. Maintainers should name a responsible enforcement contact and publish an appeal process before release.
